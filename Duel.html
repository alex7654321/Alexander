Settings

Changing the game length starts a new game.

Green: arrow keys or the bottom pad.
Red: W A S D or the top pad.

Who is playing?

Touch the ball to take it. Hit the other player and you lose it. You bounce off walls and posts and keep the ball. A player holding the ball can fly into a goal, and a free ball that rolls into a goal counts too. In the striped boxes by the goals you can move around, but you can't stop: the stop button does nothing there.

Play online

One of you hosts and the other joins with a short game code, and the game starts. If that does not work, use the long codes instead. The host is green and the guest is red, and each of you sees your own goal at the bottom.

Tell your friend this game code. The game starts when they join.
